Caveats:
- Spocket costs $39.99/mo after a 7-day trial (https://www.spocket.co/pricing). Its order integrations cover platforms like Shopify, Wix and WooCommerce, so a custom site would need manual ordering. That conflicts with the $0 budget. CJdropshipping (free, no monthly fee, accepts manual or CSV orders) is the better long-term fit, but cjdropshipping.com and aliexpress.com both put up human-verification/CAPTCHA pages for automated access, so none of their products or prices were verified.
- Spocket's public product pages hide the supplier name (codenames such as "Teal Simba") and the ship-from country. Confirm US-warehouse origin inside the account.
